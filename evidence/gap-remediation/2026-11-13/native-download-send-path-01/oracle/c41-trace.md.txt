The callback's recipient and subsequent scheduler work are not established by these eight queries.

Relation to `1000c200`: its `queued_count <= entry_allowance OR readiness` gate is on pending-send state, while the worker's `entry[6]` counts bytes **requested for serialization/enqueue**, not necessarily bytes accepted by `OTSnd`. The worker initializes allowance to 0x19000=102,400 and can grow it in 102,400-byte steps to 0x200000=2 MiB. The observed 98,304-byte TCP prefix is not any threshold shown in this path. A plausible future witness would need queue-node remaining bytes, entry allowance and requested/enqueued count, `OTSnd` acceptance, callback registration/firing, and actual worker revisits at the same reference. No historical cause is proved.
