This is a *local call-chain observation*, not proof that no outer exception handler, OS transaction, or recovery mechanism exists. A nonzero PBWriteSync result raises SError before the handler's normal ACK path, but exact externally observed error status and postfault stored bytes are not established by this trace.

## Criterion and smallest decisive fault

- Frozen GAP-072 closure criterion (`/data/leynos/Projects/hotline-protocol-docs/gap-register.json`, GAP-072) requires defining 350–353 mutation/default/**atomicity** rules with existing-password, literal-x, rename/conflict and protected-delete fixtures. Existing fixtures address those named normal/default/permission cases. This trace sharpens the mutation/error boundary but leaves **failed single-operation 353 atomicity** unmeasured. It does not justify an all-or-nothing, guaranteed-partial, durable-flush or universal error-status claim.
- A Finder Locked `UserData` file can establish the outcome of a lock-induced **open or SetEOF** failure if the phase is positively identified; it cannot alone establish whether a fault *after successful SetEOF and during PBWriteSync* leaves a resized/partial record. The smallest decisive controlled fixture is an owned account on a disposable image, verified pre-write fork bytes/catalogue identity, a fault positively placed after SetEOF and in/after PBWriteSync, exact wire ACK/error or disconnect, stopped-image postfault fork bytes/catalogue identity, and restoration. If phase placement is not provable, retain the result as bounded open/set-EOF failure and leave write atomicity open. No such fault was induced in A64.
