## A82 blocker

**Resolved for the identified failure path.** In
`scripts/native_board_control.py:183-209`, `Wire.close` now encloses
shutdown, EOF poll and residual-buffer validation in a `try` with a `finally`
that closes the exact owned socket, emits `close` (or `close_error`) and removes
the socket from `Recorder.sockets`. An exception from the parser during
`poll()` passes through this `finally`; the original `ValueError` is not
converted to success. Raw `receive` events are recorded by `poll()` before
decode and remain available to `run()`'s failure report.

`run():788-827` still catches a `Wire.close` `ValueError` during the final
owned-socket sweep, appends the tail error and sets `status="failed"` before
writing the report. A prior observation failure likewise remains failed;
the new `finally` does not turn missing ACKs or malformed frames into a quiet
success. The source diff changes only `Wire.close`, one test, and the guide.

The new `test_real_wire_tail_failures_close_owned_fd_and_preserve_bytes`
uses actual nonblocking `socket.socketpair()` sockets and the real
`Wire.close`, not `FakeWire.close`. Its two inputs exercise (1) a short
truncated frame at EOF and (2) an invalid declared size during parsing.
Both must raise `ValueError`, leave `client.fileno() == -1`, remove `sender`
from `Recorder.sockets`, retain the exact received bytes, and end with a
`close` event. Root reports the full focused 11-test suite passed; this
review inspected the test and the fixed code directly.

managed recorders, or a native server. That boundary remains a live readiness
gate, not a defect introduced by B80. The fixture still requires actual
