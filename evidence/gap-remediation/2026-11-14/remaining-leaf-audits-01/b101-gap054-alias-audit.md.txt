## Source boundary and smallest remaining fixture

`AL-D-1002f600.c` classifies unresolved aliases as kind6 after resolver failure; `AL-D-1002f310.c` calls `FollowFinderAlias` and may update/write the alias resource. The alternative `ResolveAliasFile` branch depends on `DAT_10093be9`. The retained decompilation does not prove that a genuinely missing target avoids a locate dialog or other UI interaction. Do not assume `param_5=0` is a no-UI guarantee. A 204 request is a **move to Trash**, not target removal, and cannot serve as the missing-target stimulus.

Conditional minimum is one new isolated disposable, owned-target experiment with at most six bounded cases: (1) prove a unique owned target and alias resolve live; (2) positively prove that **that exact target catalogue object is permanently absent** while preserving the alias object; (3) inspect 200 and 206 on the alias; (4) request bounded 202/210 as appropriate; (5) repeat read-only alias view after any resolver mutation; (6) verify stopped target/alias catalogue and fork identities. Pair full control/data bytes and retain all EOF/quiet tails. Positive absence must be an actual stopped-catalogue check, not merely a Files-path miss. A missing-target result must include the server/guest UI state and any changed `alis` resource bytes. Stop on a locate modal or unexpected write; do not click through by guess.

**Preparation blocker:** the current corpus and installed local tools do not establish a safe exact-object permanent-deletion mechanism. `hdel`, `hfsutils`, `hfsplus` and `hfsfuse` are absent; the existing native 204 path only moves to Trash. Finder Empty Trash would be unsafe on the previously used image, whose move corpus retained numerous Trash objects. Before implementation, provision a fresh disposable volume/image with a positively empty Trash and an independently reviewed exact-object removal procedure, or an owned guest helper that calls `FSpDelete` on that one verified target. Reconfirm its authority and read/write bounds before any case. Do not touch original Files, accounts or unrelated Trash. No closure is defensible until that fixture exists.
