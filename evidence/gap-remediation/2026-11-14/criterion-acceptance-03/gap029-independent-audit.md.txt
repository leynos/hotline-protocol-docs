## Evidence limits and disposition

A49's `native-settings-persistence-witness.md` correctly states that fresh
107 reruns the helper: baseline output alone cannot exclude other stored state.
Unchanged complete named UserData/Prefs forks plus static 304 call path make
the *controlled 304 session-only* inference stronger. The same-image phase
has changed whole-image hashes, so image equality is not claimed. A46 and A48
File > Quit, Quit Now, Finder Shut Down selections, PID absence, and FD-free
pre-extraction gate are operator observations with byte-identified screenshots
and timeline; the journal records starts but no exit status, the original
FD-free stdout was not retained, and the later receipt is retrospective.
A48 omitted `oracle.lock`, though the supervisor held the exclusive lease,
paired three flows and zero primary traffic. This is a procedure deviation,
not a contradictory protocol observation. No hard-crash, power-loss, all-storage,
121 durability experiment, or universal writer/consumer absence is proved.

**Disposition proposal to root:** the literal original criterion is defensibly
met for a scoped native 1.9 session-settings contract when it states the above
bounds and source/fixture basis. The remaining lifecycle attestation and
all-storage aspirations are not in the original criterion and should stay
explicit limits. If root requires direct post-restart 121 behaviour before
accepting “session-only” for 121, the smallest missing fixture is one delayed
121 settings update followed by the same-image orderly restart and fixed fresh
107, with retained PID/FD exit receipts. That is stronger evidence, not a
silent expansion of the frozen criterion. Root alone adjudicates closure.
