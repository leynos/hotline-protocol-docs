mtime is **not** capture-start time or trusted exposure duration. Its row,

cutoff support one finite roster continuation. They cannot prove field-115
meaning, renderer behavior, arbitrary values or long-term session stability.

partial-support outcome. The finite native producer/default rules in canonical
