## Accepted fixes

- At the end of `check`, the helper now calls `disk_snapshot` a second time
  and compares every recorded key, after rechecking unique FD6 owner. That
  repeats executable SHA-256, UID, netns, image inode and start tick before
  writing a receipt. A direct replay of B95's changed-executable probe now
  calls `exe_hash` twice and rejects the second changed hash. The new test
  also perturbs UID and namespace at the final read and requires failure.
- The capture parser now unpacks the 24-byte header according to its magic
  and requires version 2.4, zone/accuracy zero, snaplen 65535 and Ethernet
  link type 1. It rejects nanosecond magic. A direct replay of B95's
  same-inode invalid minor/snaplen/DLT counterexample now fails on version;
  new tests reject each malformed field in both byte orders and accept a
  complete big-endian sample. These are the known recorder's PCAP-header
  properties, not a claim that packet records are valid or complete.
- The guide now explicitly says the disk receipt does not establish that
  the first Ethernet packet is still pending. The B95 real-child probe with
  FD16/header already open still returns a disk identity receipt, as it
  should under root's accepted phase contract. Capture phase then checks
  current FD16/path/inode/header before protocol traffic.

## Remaining boundary

This review finds no blocker within the corrected helper's stated scope.
It does not prove that the capture phase ran before the first Hotline
request; the future orchestrator must order and attest that boundary. The
helper cannot atomically lock a process against mutation after its final
snapshot, so the existing live runtime identity and capture gates must be
rechecked immediately before protocol contact. Neither test suite nor this
review proves actual namespace/emulator integration or packet drop counts.
