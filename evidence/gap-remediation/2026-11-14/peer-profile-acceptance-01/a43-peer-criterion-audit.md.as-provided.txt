- **Original question:** “Which client builds are acceptance targets, what stable evidence distinguishes them after removing sub-version 2, and what is the unknown-peer fallback?”
- **Original close-when:** “Create a hashed client/capture corpus and evidence-based profile/fallback rules; retain reported version values separately from proven identities.”
- **Hashed corpus met:** `identified-client-source.md#full-native-peer-acceptance-matrix` pins shx source `adb1bf8…`, exact executable `8fbbe56…`, build profile, five complete native sessions, paired host/guest PCAP and negative controls. Its configured-name/controlled-receiver section adds actual native field-162 ASCII/high-byte/empty observations, exact 122 replay and SynHX-shaped subject-115 fixtures with real shx process/socket/terminal checks. `peer-tolerance-matrix.md` adds five controlled receiver sessions from that same build, including 161 omission/value and an unknown login field. `classic-native-baseline.md` and `classic-field161-matrix.md` pin acquired Classic DATA/RSRC forks, native server, actual controlled login/121/roster/212/banner stream and three 161 variants with five synchronized TCP views and full A7 gates. This is a second build profile, despite incomplete interoperability. The archive/app label 1.9.2 and embedded `vers` 1.9.1.0 conflict; both remain separate from the fork hashes and vendor authenticity remains unproved.
- **Stable distinction met:** Exact build/source/fork hashes and capture provenance identify each *observed* profile. `internal-compatibility-matrix.md#compatibility-matrix` correctly rejects shared sub-version 2 and numeric reported version as family discriminators. A wire-only classifier that recognizes an unidentified client as either build has not been established; original closure says evidence-based profile/fallback rules, not mandatory wire fingerprinting. For unverified peers the safe evidence rule is “unknown build”, with no vendor or release inference.
- **Still open as documentation/policy:** The corpus does not by itself **select** acceptance targets or state an explicit unknown-peer fallback in one contract. A precise protocol-document disposition is to name the two hashed builds as *evaluated evidence profiles*, specify that native 1.9's unconditional login 160/161 and nonempty configured 162 apply regardless inferred client family, and that 122 remains gated by the server's captured saved-client-version/configuration conditions. For unknown builds, retain `unknown` identity and describe native producer behavior; make no compatibility guarantee or mxd omission/default rule. If the term “acceptance target” means a release commitment beyond documenting 1.9, that selection is user policy, not an evidence fact. No new peer capture is intrinsically required to state this evidence-limited fallback. GAP-080 should remain Investigating until the target/fallback wording is adopted and root judges the criterion.
