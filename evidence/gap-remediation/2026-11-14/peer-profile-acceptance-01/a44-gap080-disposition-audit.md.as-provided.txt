# A44 independent GAP-080 disposition audit (read only)

**Frozen authority.** `hotline-protocol-docs/gap-register.json` GAP-080 asks: “Which client builds are acceptance targets, what stable evidence distinguishes them after removing sub-version 2, and what is the unknown-peer fallback?” It closes when there is “a hashed client/capture corpus and evidence-based profile/fallback rules; retain reported version values separately from proven identities.” This is narrower than GAP-086 release sign-off and broader than merely listing two binaries.

**Verdict.** The proposed registry and fallback **would satisfy** GAP-080's original question and close-when, provided the document actually names the two exact evaluated builds, pins their corpus hashes and finite accepted/untested cells, and states the unknown case as a rule. It does not require a vendor-wide classifier or a client-family guess from wire bytes. “Acceptance target” here must mean a documented **evidence target for native 1.9 interoperability**, not “all features passed” or an mxd release commitment. The Classic candidate's incomplete end-to-end status must appear prominently alongside its positive cells.

**No true missing evidence for this proposed GAP-080 disposition.** The required hashed source/build/capture corpus already exists, and the fallback is derivable from inspected native producer behavior without inventing a wire classifier. The work remaining is to publish the explicit registry/rule and have root judge closure against that exact text. If the document calls Classic “Hotline 1.9.2” without the label/`vers` conflict, claims vendor authenticity, equates reported version with identity, promises universal unknown-peer compatibility, or silently selects an mxd omission policy, the criterion is **not** met faithfully.

**Boundaries.** GAP-084 is already `Resolved` on identified peer/core-session captures, expected events, reproducible runner and packet provenance; its closure explicitly separates wider operation support and release selection. GAP-081 still tracks per-field acceptance matrix cells and any omission/default choice. GAP-086 remains `Investigating` on actual release subset/owner/sign-off/proof decisions. The user has held further mxd validation; this protocol registry must not restart it or choose an mxd release target. No status or tracked file was changed in this audit.
