## Disposition

No deletion is proposed or authorised by this audit. Empty
`debug/incremental` cannot reclaim space. A hardlink-free compiled subtree
large enough to meet the current capacity deficit was not established.

executables; smaller dylint/trybuild caches require separate path-specific
owner and regeneration proof and do not individually close the deficit.
Continuing host writes make the free-space observation unstable.
