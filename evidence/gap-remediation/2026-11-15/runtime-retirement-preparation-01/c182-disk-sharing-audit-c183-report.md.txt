# C183 stopped runtime disk FIEMAP census

Read-only XFS metadata snapshot, 2026-11-14 UTC. This extends C182 to the
`base.dsk` and `working.dsk` in every A192 FD-free directory: 20 stopped
candidate directories plus protected `native-board-02` as a reference.
It includes historical `hotline19`, `hotline20`, `hotline21`, and `install9`.
It excludes the three actual live-image directories and the directory
`native-rate304-03`, which still has active bridge/log holders. No disk/ROM
contents were read; no dedupe, copy, unlink, service, or VM action occurred.

## Method and scope

`extent_census.py` (143 lines) pins A192's exact 25-directory inventory,
checks every sampled file's device/inode/size/UID against A192, requires a
current root `fuser -a` no-holder result for each file, then saves the
`/usr/sbin/filefrag -v` FIEMAP extent map. It checks identity again after
the ioctl. Its `fiemap-all/` text files and `c183-summary.json` retain all
42 maps, per-file `st_blocks`, `shared` and unshared extents, directory
physical unions, and overlaps. This is a point-in-time holder/extent check.

| Extent view over 42 sampled disks | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Sum of per-file `st_blocks`, as `du` would count | 314.711 GiB |
| Sum of mapped extents | 314.711 GiB |
| Physical interval union within the sample | 15.655 GiB |
| Repeated per-file counting within the sample | 299.056 GiB |
| All FIEMAP-unshared intervals, including protected reference | 8.066 MiB |
| Unshared intervals in 20 candidate directories | 7.750 MiB |

The sampled native experiments' physical maps largely overlap the
authoritative board02 donor. Every candidate base has zero unshared blocks.
All 7.750 MiB of FIEMAP-unshared candidate data are in stopped working
images. Most have 40–328 KiB; `native-banlab-three-01/working.dsk` has
4.012 MiB. These are currently exclusive data extents and a plausible
data-block reclaim floor if those particular files were removed, subject
to a fresh identity/holder check and retention approval. They may be
important guest changes; uniqueness is a preservation warning, not a
deletion recommendation.

Historical `install9` has 7.812 GiB of physical intervals not overlapping
the other sampled disks, and `hotline19` has about 20 MiB of sample-only
intervals. **All of those intervals are FIEMAP-shared**, so there are other
references outside this 42-file sample. The historical installation/source
chain is protected independently of block sharing. The protected board02
working image itself has about 0.316 MiB of unshared data; it is not a
cleanup candidate.

## Reclaim and deduplication decision

The 314.711 GiB `du` total is not a physical reclaim forecast. For these
42 disk files, repeated/shared mappings account for about 299.056 GiB of
the per-file count. Removing sampled native base copies would free no
mapped data blocks while their protected donor remains. Removing all
stopped experiment working images would release at least their 7.750 MiB
of currently unshared data blocks, plus inode/extent metadata; the census
does not establish a larger reclaim amount. Shared extents could become
reclaimable only if every other reference were removed, including references
outside this sample, which is neither measured nor authorised.

`xfs_io dedupe` compares source and destination bytes in the kernel before
remapping. At most the observed unshared working extents offer immediate
data-block dedupe potential here, and their content may intentionally differ.
There is no evidence of a multi-GiB dedupe opportunity in the stopped
experiments. Dedupe would mutate extent mappings and require separate
full-content, inode, timestamp, mode, provenance and FD-free gates; none is
proposed. The audit does not measure ROMs, PCAPs, logs, other runtime files,
or system-wide XFS reference counts. Future host writes can change sharing.

The bounded raw evidence is `c183-summary.json` and `fiemap-all/`; the
earlier `report.md` describes the XFS/FIEMAP/FIDEDUPERANGE local interfaces.
No runtime directory is authorised for cleanup by this report.
