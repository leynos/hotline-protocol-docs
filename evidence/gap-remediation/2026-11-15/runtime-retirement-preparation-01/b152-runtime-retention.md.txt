Read-only metadata/document review, 2026-11-14. No ROM or disk image bytes were
read; no file was changed or removed. All 25 runtime `base.dsk` files and 25
`working.dsk` files are distinct inodes (`find state/mac-runtime -maxdepth 2`

## Working images and acceptance dependencies

- Preserve the live original `hotline22-recovery/working.dsk` and isolated
  client `hotline-client-isolated-01/working.dsk`; the [delegated plan](../../docs/execplans/delegated-gap-investigations-batch-02.md)
  identifies both as active instances and forbids reading live working bytes.
  Verify current liveness separately before any operation.
- Keep stopped `native-board-02/working.dsk`: its [board witness](../../../hotline-protocol-docs/native-board-witness.md)
  ties the 65,629-byte stopped board to twelve captured changes. Keep
  `native-path-fields-01/working.dsk`: the [structured path plan](../../docs/execplans/structured-path-fields.md)
  and portable corpus bind stopped HFS catalogue/fork checks to the 12-cell
  result. Retain the specific working images behind stopped ban and settings
  witnesses until their runtime path/inode is independently mapped; the
  [expiry witness](../../../hotline-protocol-docs/native-ban-expiry-later-witness.md)
  and [settings witness](../../../hotline-protocol-docs/native-settings-persistence-witness.md)
  give fork and image hashes, but these pages do not bind those hashes to the
  `native-ban-expiry-01` or `native-configured-apply-01` path names.
- Preserve `native-alias-live-01..04/working.dsk` as quarantined failed or
  partial UI/alias states; the [alias plan](../../docs/execplans/missing-target-alias.md)
  explicitly retains the first three stopped working copies and records the
  fourth's abort. No later positive alias binding or missing-target result
  makes them interchangeable. The protocol acceptance still needs a future
  *new* image; no earlier stopped copy supplies that result.
- Other stopped `working.dsk` files (rate304, roster, liveness, board01,
  banlab, eventlab, client candidate, hotline19–21, install9) have distinct
  inodes and potentially distinct guest writes even if a preboot hash equalled
  the base. Some have retained stopped-fork or failure evidence. No complete
  poststop byte-equality inventory was found in this bounded review. Treat
  them as recovery evidence pending a per-runtime corpus/dependency audit;
  **none is certified reclaimable here**.

## Reclamation decision

Prioritize the 20 recorded-`e5cbbaaf…` duplicate **base** candidates, not
working images, but only after C182 measures physical exclusivity/reflink
sharing and an independent no-owner/current-hash/reference review. Retain
`hotline22-recovery/base.dsk` as the canonical source. The immediate
acceptance gaps that depend on exact stopped bytes are board postconditions,
path-field stopped HFS identities, ban/settings restart state, and alias
failure/partial-state forensics. Portable fork/capture evidence does not
recreate an entire historical working image.
